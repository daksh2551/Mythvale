# Mythvale — Elite Champions Combat Upgrade

This build adds five optional elite Arcane Champions across the later realms. They have increased health, a gold crown marker, dedicated health bars, and a faster ranged spell cadence than ordinary sentinels. Existing systems and saved progression format are preserved.

Validation: source files are synchronized; JavaScript syntax and ZIP integrity should be checked before building. Android device testing has not been performed.


## Enemy Loot Drops upgrade
Defeated enemies now drop temporary glowing vitality or mana orbs. Collect vitality to restore one health point, or mana orbs to restore 35 mana. Guardians always drop vitality; regular enemies can drop either type. Drops expire after 18 seconds. This is an additional combat reward loop and does not alter saved progress formats.


## Guardian Armor Progression
Defeat 5, 15, and 30 enemies to unlock three armor tiers. Each tier increases maximum vitality and provides a 12% per-tier chance to block incoming damage. Armor progress is saved locally.
